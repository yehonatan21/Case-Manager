description: Analyse what a decision does to costs, cash and runway
argument-hint: <decision, e.g. "hiring one more case manager at 17,000 a month">
---

Use the financial-analyst subagent to analyse the budget impact of: $ARGUMENTS

Cover:
1. Total cost, one-time and monthly
2. The effect on monthly costs and, where cash is known, on runway (months)
3. Payback: the extra paid cases a month needed to cover it, at the recent average payment
4. Alternatives worth considering
5. Risks and assumptions, marked as such

Use clear sections with specific numbers, and end with a recommendation.
