---
description: Check team capacity, workload balance and service levels
argument-hint: "[focus, e.g. \"before the holiday season\" or \"citizenship cases\"]"
---

Use the operations-analyst subagent to scan the team's capacity and service levels. Focus: $ARGUMENTS

Report on:
1. Demand against throughput: new requests and completed cases a month, and whether the backlog is growing
2. Workload per team member: open cases, open and overdue tasks, and how even the load is
3. Service: first-response time against the SLA, time to quote, time to complete
4. Bottlenecks: the statuses where cases wait longest or pile up
5. Whether the team needs another person now, later or not at all, and the cheaper fixes to try first

End with three concrete actions, most important first.
