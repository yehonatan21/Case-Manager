description: Board-style brief on a decision, combining the financial and operational views
argument-hint: <topic, e.g. "launching a business-setup package">
---

Write a strategic brief on: $ARGUMENTS

Ask the financial-analyst and operations-analyst subagents for their views, then combine them:

## Summary
- The recommendation in one or two sentences
- The numbers that matter most

## Financial view (financial-analyst)
- Investment or cost, one-time and monthly
- Payback and the effect on runway
- Financial risks and how to limit them

## Operational view (operations-analyst)
- What it asks of the team and the case pipeline
- Capacity now and whether anyone needs to be hired
- Effect on service levels (SLA, time to quote, time to complete)

## Recommendation
- The plan, with milestones and dates
- How success will be measured
- What would make you change course

## Alternatives
- At least two other options, with the pros and cons of each

Where the views conflict, say so and explain which way you lean. Use `scripts/decision-matrix.ts` if comparing options helps.
