Your whole case in one place: from the first request until the service is done – clear and transparent.

Describe what you need in your own words
Write it like a conversation. A smart assistant asks only what's missing, and you always see what we understood. Attach files and photos, or use a regular form if you prefer.

The next step is always clear
Open your case and you see one thing: the most urgent thing needed from you, in plain words, with a button that does it. Need to send a document? Photograph it with your phone and upload it right there.

Quote, approval and payment
Get a detailed quote, ask a question or approve it and sign it digitally. Pay by card on a secure payment page and get a receipt or invoice in your case.

Follow your case
Work stages with target dates, a timeline of everything that happened, messages with the team, the documents you sent and the ones you received – all in one case. You also see who's handling it and how quickly they usually reply.

Notifications on your phone
A new message, a quote, a document needed or an update on your case arrives as a notification, even when the app is closed. Tapping it opens exactly the right place.

Signatures and meetings
Sign a power of attorney or an engagement letter right from your phone. Book a consultation (video, phone or at the office) in a free slot of the person handling your case, and add it to your calendar.

Simple, secure sign-in
No passwords: get a code by email, or continue with Google. Files are stored encrypted, and every download is checked and logged.

Hebrew and English
The app is in Hebrew (right to left) and switches to English in one tap. Notifications and emails arrive in the language you chose.

For the office team
The same app serves the team: a dashboard of what needs attention now, my cases, tasks, client conversations, quotes, payments and meetings – on the phone too.

Accessibility
Built to WCAG 2.2 level AA, with screen reader and keyboard support.
